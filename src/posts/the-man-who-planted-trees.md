I recently read the short story *"The Man Who Planted Trees"* by Jean Giono. It is a short and easy read but well worth the time. It is a story about a young man meeting a shepard who is spending his time replanting trees is a desolate valley. The young man meets him multiple times throughout his life and each time the trees grow bigger and the treeles valley slowly becomes home to a flourishing forest. This return of nature attracts people who settle there. The vibrant village full of life and joy creates a stark contrast for the young man who many years ago couldn't even find drinkable water in this area. 

The story is a pleasant read. It can resonate deeply with our own ability for ecological regeneration. It is also encouraging and empowering since small actions done continuously can bring about meaningful change. Even a simple individual, without extraordinary talents or power, can change a little corner of the world to be better than it was before. Even though the book's theme is connected with ecology, the message can apply to any area. It can motivate us that any action, no matter how small, is a step in the right direction.

The book can be found for free online since it is royalty-free. There is also an animated movie based on the book available with both [English](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=epTqUnKsuUY) and [French](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yKhdij8D770) narration.
